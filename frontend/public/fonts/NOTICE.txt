The Exam Sans TC and Exam Serif TC WOFF2 files in this site are subsets of
Noto Sans CJK TC and Noto Serif CJK TC, respectively. They were generated
from the fonts-noto-cjk package using scripts/build_frontend_fonts.py.
The font family names were changed for these modified subsets.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
